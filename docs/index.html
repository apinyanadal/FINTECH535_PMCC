Cumulative P&L

Daily mark-to-market P&L of each book, all starting from the same cash. The short calls are identical in the covered call and the PMCC, so the gap between those two lines is entirely the LEAP vs the shares (plus interest on the PMCC's idle cash).

Stock and the strikes sold: expired vs assigned

Daily close of the stock. Each bar is one weekly call from sale to expiry at its strike: blue = expired worthless, coral = finished in the money (bought back). The dotted line is the LEAP strike.

Weekly short-call P&L

Premium collected minus the buyback cost, per contract-week. Small, steady gains in quiet weeks; large losses in the weeks the stock rallied through the strike.

Where the PMCC's P&L came from

Exposure at each sale: share-equivalents

Measured at the close each weekly call was sold. PMCC = 100 x (LEAP delta - short call delta); covered call = 100 x (1 - short call delta). It shows how much of a $1 stock move each position keeps, up or down.
